# PL-300 Study Lab

A 30-day study site for Microsoft exam PL-300 (Power BI Data Analyst), mapped to the skills measured as of April 20, 2026.

- **Learn**: a primer for each of the 11 skill groups, written for someone starting from zero, plus lesson zero (setup).
- **Practice**: 141 original questions and 3 case studies in the exam's formats (single and multiple choice, yes/no series, DAX drop-downs, ordering), each tagged to an official skill. Adaptive mode targets weak and untested skills and repeats misses.
- **Diagnostic and mock exams**: a 24-question diagnostic and 50-question, 100-minute mocks scored out of 1000.
- **AI tutor** (bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI key): teaches each skill from zero, explains mistakes, writes fresh questions for weak spots. The key stays in your browser.
- **Three finance capstones** (beginner, intermediate, advanced) with generated datasets and checkpoints verified against the data.
- **Readiness gate**: diagnostic taken, every skill group at 75%+, all capstones complete, and the two latest mocks at 800+.

Progress is stored in the browser and can be exported to a file and imported on another device.

All questions, case studies and datasets are original; company names are fictional. This is an independent study tool and isn't affiliated with Microsoft. Real exam questions are confidential and aren't used here.
